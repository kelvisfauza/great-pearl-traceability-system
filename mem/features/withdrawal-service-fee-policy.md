---
name: Withdrawal Service Fee Policy
description: Tiered withdrawal/mobile-send service fee applies to GosentePay payouts only (under UGX 50,000); Yo Payments payouts are fee-free
type: feature
---
Tiered service fee (1,100 / 1,700 / 2,500 / 2,900) applies **only** to GosentePay payouts, i.e. amounts under UGX 50,000 in `instant-withdrawal` and `SendMoneyModal` mobile sends.

Yo Payments payouts (amounts >= UGX 50,000) carry **no** service fee — do not charge or display one.
